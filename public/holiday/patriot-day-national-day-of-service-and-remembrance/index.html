Patriot Day — National Day of Service & Remembrance

Celebration

Patriot Day, observed every September 11, honors the nearly 3,000 people killed in the terrorist attacks of September 11, 2001. Flags are flown at half-staff, and Americans are encouraged to mark the day with a moment of silence and acts of community service.
