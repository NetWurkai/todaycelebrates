Labor Day

Celebration

Labor Day is a U.S. federal holiday held on the first Monday of September that honors the American labor movement and the contributions workers have made to the country's strength and prosperity. First celebrated by trade unions in the 1880s, it became a national holiday in 1894 and today doubles as the unofficial end of summer.
