    National Fried Chicken Sandwich Day falls on November 9, and as far as any reliable record shows it is a marketing occasion rather than a commemoration: no founding organization, proclamation or first observance can be traced for it. What it points at is real enough. The fried chicken sandwich is the most contested item in American fast food, and the question of who invented it has never been settled by anyone without a stake in the answer.

    History

    The origin of this observance is not well documented. No founding body, government page or news report establishes who created National Fried Chicken Sandwich Day, or when it was first marked. The date circulates through daily-holiday aggregators and through restaurant promotions — Jollibee, for one, offered its rewards members a one-day buy-one-get-one chicken sandwich on November 9, 2026 — but a chain running a deal is evidence that the day is observed, not evidence of where it came from.

    The sandwich has a better-documented history, and a genuine dispute inside it. Chick-fil-A says its founder, S. Truett Cathy, created the recipe for the Original Chicken Sandwich — a boneless breast, two pickles, a toasted buttery bun — in 1964, at the Dwarf Grill in Hapeville, Georgia, and that the company has used the same recipe ever since. The Georgia Historical Society marker standing at that site is more cautious: it says Cathy "invented what is widely considered the first-ever fried chicken breast sandwich in the early 1960s." The marker was also erected in 2017 by the Georgia Historical Society and Chick-fil-A, Inc. jointly, so it is not an account independent of the company.

    The firm dates in the story are the restaurant's rather than the recipe's. The marker gives May 23, 1946 as the day Cathy opened the Dwarf Grill, later renamed the Dwarf House, at 461 N. Central Avenue in Hapeville. The first Chick-fil-A restaurant followed in 1967, in Atlanta's Greenbriar Mall.

    The sandwich became a national event in 2019. Popeyes put its chicken sandwich on menus nationwide on August 12 at $3.99. A Chick-fil-A post days later, claiming its own version as the original, turned the launch into a public argument. On August 27, Popeyes announced it had sold out — having forecast demand through the end of September, it had sold through that inventory in about two weeks.

    The shift underneath all this is older than any chain. Per-person availability of chicken in the United States passed beef in 2010, according to USDA's Economic Research Service, and by 2021 stood at 68.1 pounds against beef's 56.2.

    Timeline

    • May 23, 1946 — S. Truett Cathy opens the Dwarf Grill, later the Dwarf House, at 461 N. Central Avenue in Hapeville, Georgia.
    • Early 1960s — Cathy develops the fried chicken breast sandwich at that restaurant. Chick-fil-A dates the finished recipe to 1964; the historical marker at the site says only "the early 1960s."
    • 1967 — The first Chick-fil-A restaurant opens in Atlanta's Greenbriar Mall.
    • 2010 — Per-person availability of chicken in the United States passes that of beef for the first time.

    • August 27, 2019 — Popeyes announces the sandwich has sold out, having forecast demand through the end of September.

    How to observe

    Read the Georgia Historical Society marker text instead of the brand copy. It is public, and the distance between "widely considered" the first "in the early 1960s" and "created the recipe in 1964" is a small lesson in how a founding story firms up as it is retold.

    If you are buying one, the two variables that actually decide the sandwich are whether the chicken is a whole breast or a formed patty, and whether the bun is toasted in fat. Both are normally stated on a chain's own ingredient or nutrition pages, which is the only place the answer is reliable.

    And check the chain's own site before going out for a deal. November 9 offers are run brand by brand, are often one day only, and are frequently limited to app or rewards members — terms that appear on the restaurant's page, not on the calendars listing the day.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is National Fried Chicken Sandwich Day?

    November 9, every year. It is sometimes listed as National Chicken Sandwich Day.

    Who started National Fried Chicken Sandwich Day?

    No reliable source names a founder, a sponsoring organization or a first observance. Any specific origin story you find for this day is unsourced.

    Who invented the fried chicken sandwich?

    It is disputed, and the two main accounts are not independent of each other. Chick-fil-A credits its founder, S. Truett Cathy, and dates the recipe to 1964. The Georgia Historical Society marker at the original restaurant says only "the early 1960s" and calls the sandwich "widely considered" the first — and that marker was erected jointly with Chick-fil-A, Inc.

    What happened with the Popeyes chicken sandwich in 2019?

    Popeyes launched it nationwide on August 12, 2019 at $3.99. On August 27 the company announced it had sold out, having forecast demand through the end of September.

    Do restaurants run deals on November 9?

    Some do, brand by brand. Jollibee, for example, offered rewards members a buy-one-get-one chicken sandwich on November 9, 2026. There is no central program, so offers have to be checked on each chain's own site.
