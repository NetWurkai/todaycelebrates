    World Kindness Day is observed on November 13 by the World Kindness Movement, the international NGO that created it and launched it on that date in 1998. It is one of the few observances of its kind with a traceable founding organization and a documented start, and the movement's own account is unusually specific about both. What it asks for is deliberately unstructured: do something kind, and let it count.

    History

    The World Kindness Movement dates its formation to a conference in Tokyo on September 20, 1997, when Japan brought together kindness organizations from several countries for the first time. The movement's own milestone list records that date as the "Declaration to establish The World Kindness Movement in Tokyo, Japan." The first World Kindness Day followed on November 13, 1998. The movement describes what it was at that point plainly: a coalition of national NGOs with a primary focus on kindness.

    The organization took longer to become an organization. It was launched publicly in Singapore on November 18, 2000, at the third WKM conference, and it did not acquire legal standing until 2019, when it registered as an NGO under Swiss law. It now counts members in more than 27 nations — among them Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, France, India and Italy — and describes itself as having no political or religious affiliation. In 2017 its Global General Assembly in Korea produced a Declaration of Support for a Kinder World, signed at the DMZ in Seoul.

    One thing the movement's own pages do not supply is a list of names. A section headed "Founding Fathers 1997" carries photographs and nothing else, so the organization that invented World Kindness Day is sourceable and its founders are not.

    Timeline

    • September 20, 1997 — Japan convenes kindness organizations from several countries in Tokyo. The World Kindness Movement dates its formation to this conference and records the day as a declaration to establish the movement.
    • November 13, 1998 — The first World Kindness Day is launched by the movement, then a coalition of national NGOs focused on kindness.
    • November 18, 2000 — The World Kindness Movement is formally launched in Singapore, at its third conference.
    • 2017 — The Declaration of Support for a Kinder World is signed at the DMZ in Seoul during the movement's Global General Assembly in Korea.
    • 2019 — The movement registers as an NGO under Swiss law and becomes a legal entity.
    • March 20, 2025 — The World Happiness Report devotes its annual edition to caring and sharing, reporting that people systematically underestimate the kindness of others.

    How to observe

    Aim at a stranger rather than at someone you already like. The 2025 World Happiness Report — published by the Wellbeing Research Centre at the University of Oxford with Gallup and the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network — gave its edition over to caring and sharing, and reported that in 2024 helping strangers remained above its 2017-19 level in every global region, by a global average of 18 percent.

    Correct your estimate of other people. The same report's key findings include that "the return rate of lost wallets is much higher than people expect," and that across countries expected wallet return significantly predicts the actual return of wallets dropped in experiments. It also found that expecting kindness from others predicts happiness more strongly than major actual or expected harms. The pessimism, in other words, is both wrong and self-confirming, and November 13 is a reasonable day to test it.

    If you would rather have something organized, the Random Acts of Kindness Foundation publishes materials for November 13 and runs a separate day of its own, Random Acts of Kindness Day, in February.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is World Kindness Day the same as Random Acts of Kindness Day?

    No. World Kindness Day is November 13 and belongs to the World Kindness Movement. The Random Acts of Kindness Foundation marks its own Random Acts of Kindness Day separately, on February 17.

    Who started World Kindness Day?

    The World Kindness Movement, which launched it on November 13, 1998. No individual founder can be named from the movement's own history: its "Founding Fathers 1997" section shows photographs without names.

    Is this a United Nations day?

    No. The day belongs to the World Kindness Movement, an international NGO registered under Swiss law since 2019 which describes itself as having no political or religious affiliation.

    Is November 13 a day off?

    No. World Kindness Day is not a federal holiday in the United States. Offices, banks, schools and post offices operate normally.

    Does being kind actually do anything measurable?

    The 2025 World Happiness Report found that expecting kindness from others predicts happiness more strongly than major actual or expected harms, and that helping strangers worldwide stayed roughly 18 percent above its 2017-19 level in 2024.
